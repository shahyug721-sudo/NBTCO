A Mumbai-based dealer and supplier of industrial fasteners and bearings, operating from Nagdevi in the heart of the city's hardware market.

Established in 1996, New Bharat Trading Company supplies nuts, bolts, screws, washers, socket set screws, high-tensile and stainless-steel fasteners, ball bearings and steel balls — backed by genuine quality, fair pricing and quick dispatch.

Over nearly three decades we have built long-term relationships by keeping a wide ready stock and helping customers select the right grade, standard and finish for their joint. As an authorised stockist of Unbrako — the world leader in socket screws and Durlok self-locking fasteners — we supply performance-critical fasteners that meet or exceed DIN, ISO, ASTM and IS standards.
